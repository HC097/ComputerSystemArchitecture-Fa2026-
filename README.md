# Computer System Architecture Lecture Notes

This repository contains lecture notes, worked examples, and supporting materials for **Computer System Architecture**.

The notes are organized around the topics covered in the course and are designed to accompany a corresponding series of recorded lectures.

## Primary Reference

The principal textbook used in preparing these notes is:

**M. Morris Mano, _Computer System Architecture_, Third Edition.**  
Prentice Hall, 1993.

The notes are intended to explain, organize, and teach the material rather than reproduce the textbook verbatim. Terminology, concepts, examples, and organization generally follow the treatment presented by Mano unless otherwise stated.

---

## Lecture Note Philosophy

These notes are designed around a simple rule:

> **If we speak, we write.**

Anything important enough to explain during a lecture should also have a written counterpart that students can record and review later.

This includes:

- definitions,
- equations,
- procedures,
- worked examples,
- diagrams,
- comparisons,
- important observations,
- and summary statements.

The goal is to avoid an invisible layer of lecture material that exists only in the spoken presentation.
